CookieMop

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026
The short version: CookieMop collects nothing. Everything happens locally in your browser. We have no servers, no analytics, no accounts, and no ads — so there is no data we could collect, sell, or lose.

What CookieMop does

CookieMop automatically deletes cookies and site data for websites when you close their tabs, except for sites you whitelist. All processing happens entirely on your device, inside your browser.

Data we collect

None. CookieMop does not collect, transmit, store remotely, sell, or share any personal data or browsing data. The extension makes no network requests of any kind.

There are none. No analytics SDKs, no error trackers, no advertising networks, no external fonts or scripts.
