CookieMop

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026 · Updated for Pro: August 21, 2026
The short version: CookieMop collects nothing. Everything happens locally in your browser. We have no servers, no analytics, no accounts, and no ads — so there is no data we could collect, sell, or lose.

What CookieMop does

CookieMop automatically deletes cookies and site data for websites when you close their tabs, except for sites you whitelist. All processing happens entirely on your device, inside your browser.

Data we collect

None. CookieMop does not collect, transmit, store remotely, sell, or share any personal data or browsing data. The extension makes no network requests of any kind.

If you buy CookieMop Pro

Payment is handled by Lemon Squeezy, who act as merchant of record and receive your payment and billing details directly — we never see your card. They also send you the receipt containing your order number.

To get your license key you enter that email address and order number on our license page. Those two values are used only to verify the order with Lemon Squeezy, and they are not stored — there is no database. The key is derived from them, so looking it up again always returns the same key.

The extension itself still makes no network requests, even with Pro. Your license key is verified locally on your device against a public key built into the extension. There is no license server, no activation call, and no usage reporting. Pro works offline, and it keeps working even if our payment infrastructure disappears.

Third parties

Inside the extension there are none. No analytics SDKs, no error trackers, no advertising networks, no external fonts or scripts. Lemon Squeezy handles payment, and is involved only if you choose to buy Pro — and only outside the extension.
