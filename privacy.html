CookieMop Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

CookieMop collects no data. Nothing you do ever leaves your browser.

What CookieMop does

CookieMop automatically deletes cookies and site data for sites you visit when you close their tabs, except for sites you whitelist. All of this happens locally, inside your browser, using Chrome's extension APIs.

Data collection

  • No personal data is collected. CookieMop has no servers and makes no network requests.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no ads, no accounts.
  • No browsing history is transmitted anywhere. The list of sites a tab has visited is kept temporarily in Chrome's local session storage, only so the extension knows which cookies to clean, and is discarded when the tab closes.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings and site rules (whitelist/greylist, delay, cleanup scope) are stored in Chrome's extension storage. If you are signed into Chrome, Chrome may sync them between your own devices — this is Chrome's built-in sync, not a CookieMop server.
  • Cleaning statistics (a simple counter of cookies removed) are stored locally on your device only.

Permissions

CookieMop requests cookie, tab, browsing-data, storage and alarm permissions solely to perform its single purpose: deleting cookies and site data for sites you don't trust. A detailed permission-by-permission explanation is in the README.

Open source

The complete source code is public at github.com/thoopring/cookiemop — the store build is produced from that exact code, so anyone can verify these claims.

Changes & contact

If this policy ever changes, the update will appear on this page with a new date. Questions or concerns: open a GitHub issue.
